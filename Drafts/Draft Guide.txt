SMART PANTRY - DRAFT GUIDE

The draft files are grouped by the week they best represent in the development of Smart Pantry.
Some later milestone versions were reconstructed from the development history to show the progression clearly.
The final submitted program is outside this folder as Smart_Pantry_Final.py.

WEEK 1 - Starting the program
- Main Menu.py
  First simple menu and navigation structure.

WEEK 2 - Building the inventory basics
- Sleep Mode.py
  Added the early sleep/wake idea.
- Basic Inventory.py
  Began storing food information.
- Add and View Items.py
  Added the first usable Add Item and View Items flow.
- Input Checks.py
  Added basic checks for quantities and prices.

WEEK 3 - Dates, messages and safer adding
- Expiry Tracking.py
  Added date comparison and expiry statuses.
- Expiry Messages.py
  Added expiry warnings/messages and an update page.
- Add Item Cancel and Edit.py
  Added cancel, confirmation and edit-before-saving behaviour.

WEEK 4 - Safer editing
- Safer Updates.py
  Added confirmation before updating or deleting items.

WEEK 5 - Multiple batches and categories
- Multiple Batches and Clear Messages.py
  Allowed the same food to exist in separate expiry batches and improved message clearing.
- Food Categories.py
  Added Fridge/Fresh, Pantry and Frozen organisation.

WEEK 6 - Shopping features
- Shopping List.py
  Added the first shopping-list system.
- Shopping Recommendations.py
  Added regular items, minimum stock and low-stock recommendations.

WEEK 7 - Budget, units and household features
- Budget.py
  Added the first grocery-budget calculations.
- Shopping and Budget Combined.py
  Connected shopping-list costs with budget information.
- KG Pricing.py
  Added per-item and per-kilogram pricing.
- Meal Board.py
  Added the persistent lunch/dinner attendance board.
- Meal Messages and Reset.py
  Added meal notifications and next-day reset behaviour.

WEEK 8 - Tkinter transition
- Tkinter Start.py
  First graphical main window and inventory view.
- Tkinter Full Version.py
  Converted the wider prototype to a basic Tkinter interface.
